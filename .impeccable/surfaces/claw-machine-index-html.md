# Surface: 인형뽑기 가게 (claw-machine/index.html)

Mode: experience — the game itself leads from the first viewport.
Scope: the whole single-file game (wheel, machine, cubbies, popups, teacher settings). Replacement visual world; product truth, content, function and user-pinned constraints preserved (wheel on the LEFT, "성공~~~!" / "꽝~~!", Sanrio-style dolls, lesson-plan lines and roles).
Audience & job: 12 pre-reading children + their mothers at a shared classroom screen (2–4 m), teacher driving. Job per turn: wheel picks child → child+mum tap a doll → claw grabs → prize lands in the child's own cubby; repeat until all present children succeed.
Constraints: touch-first, one file, no network needed (fonts inlined), legible from the back row, nothing a child taps may steal a turn or reset play.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The screen is 새들1반's own birch-plywood cubby unit turned into a shop; a prize is not a popup, it is a doll placed in your cubby. Refuses the floating cartoon machine on a pastel wall with sidebar cards.
OWN-WORLD: Birch plywood face and layered ply edge, rounded corners; cubby backs in classroom-bin colours (sunflower, sky, leaf, tomato, lilac, pink); laminated white name tags with a personal picture symbol; EVA puzzle-mat floor; a hand-cut paper sign, each syllable a different colour; plum ink outlines everywhere; the machine a glossy strawberry toy with a sky-blue glass.
STORY: Everyone sees whose turn it is, from the wheel and the face-out name tag, then sees the doll travel from claw to door to cubby, and watches the class's cubbies fill.
FIRST VIEWPORT: One furniture unit edge to edge. Left bay holds the spinner with the big spin button. The centre bay, about 45% of the width, holds the machine with an LED cue line. The right bay holds 12 cubbies (3x4) with tags. The handmade sign runs on the top rail, and the flip scoreboard sits in the cubby bay header.
FORM: classroom cubby unit, list position 3, seed 1cb84052.
Raises: card anatomy for every tag (wax pack); the current customer's tag promoted face-out (pulp rack); fixed-position flip counter (nixie); arrival as an event (ANSI); one active glow only (film); primary/secondary/supporting triangle with the glass sky as charged emptiness (ikebana).
Signature interaction: tag flies cubby→customer slot on pick; doll flies door→cubby on success.
